Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QuantumConfusion ("we", "us", or "the Game") handles your information. By playing the Game, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

QuantumConfusion is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to play.

The Game may collect non-identifiable technical data including:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • In-game performance metrics (e.g., frame rate, crash reports).
  • Gameplay statistics (e.g., high scores, levels reached).

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Monitor and improve game performance and stability.
  • Debug technical issues and crashes.
  • Analyze gameplay balance and difficulty.

3. Data Storage

Your game progress, including high scores and credits, is stored locally on your device using native storage. If you delete the Game, this data may be permanently removed unless backed up by your device's system-level backup services (e.g., Google Drive/iCloud).

4. Third-Party Services

The Game is built using the Capacitor framework and may utilize standard platform services provided by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS) for billing or analytics. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
